From the household of Smt. Rukmini and Sri Venkabhatta, ten children carried their legacy into the world.

Spread across cities and lives, each branch rooted in its own ground, yet none of us ever truly apart.

When we meet, it feels like no time has passed — because in the ways that matter, it hasn't.

Welcome to this small window into our world — the Nisarani family. We take our name from Nisarani, a quiet village nestled in Sorabha taluk, Shivamogga district, in the heart of Karnataka. It is where our roots were planted, where our story began, and where the name we carry was born.
